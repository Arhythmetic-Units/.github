# Sound outside the lines.

Independent music technology. Instruments and tools for electronic music,
from a one-person company working in synthesis, mixing, and sound analysis.

[Explore the website](https://arhythmetic-units.com/) ·
[Downloads & manuals](https://arhythmetic-units.com/downloads/) ·
[Research](https://arhythmetic-units.com/research/) ·
[Support](https://arhythmetic-units.com/support/)

## Free for VCV Rack

| Instrument / tool | Explore |
| --- | --- |
| [Fourier](https://arhythmetic-units.com/products/fourier/) | Spectrum analysis and spectrograms for your patch. |
| [Potato Chips](https://arhythmetic-units.com/products/potatochips/) | Classic sound-chip synthesis in a modular instrument. |
| [RackNES](https://arhythmetic-units.com/products/racknes/) | A voltage-controlled NES emulator for VCV Rack. |

Find installation links and documentation on the
[downloads page](https://arhythmetic-units.com/downloads/).

## In development

- **[Maw](https://arhythmetic-units.com/products/maw/)** — A vocal VST3 synthesizer.
- **[Quadra](https://arhythmetic-units.com/products/quadra/)** — DJ software for macOS.

Maw and Quadra do not have public downloads yet. Follow their product pages for
availability and previews.
